# Creative Angles

Intempt's 14 creative angle system for ad and content creation. Each angle is a strategic approach to presenting products, services, or brand messages visually and conceptually.

---

## 14 Creative Angles

### 1. Product Card

Clean product presentation on a solid or gradient background with specifications overlaid.

**Visual direction:** Minimalist, high-key lighting, product centered at 60-70% frame, specs as clean typographic overlays.
**Copy focus:** Product name, key specification, price point, availability.
**Best for:** Product launches, catalog-style ads, retargeting with specific products.
**Example:** A SaaS product card might show a clean UI screenshot on a gradient background with "50+ integrations | Real-time analytics | Start free" overlaid.

### 2. Price Drop

Original price crossed out with the new price shown prominently. Urgency element (timer, "limited time," stock level) drives action.

**Visual direction:** Bold typography, red/orange accents for urgency, strikethrough on original price, countdown or urgency badge.
**Copy focus:** Original price, new price, savings amount or percentage, deadline.
**Best for:** Promotions, flash sales, clearance, seasonal discounts, renewal incentives.
**Example:** "$299/mo ~~$499/mo~~ — Save 40% | Offer ends Friday"

### 3. Full-Bleed Hero

Edge-to-edge product or lifestyle image with a bold headline overlaid. Maximum visual impact.

**Visual direction:** Dramatic, immersive photography filling the entire frame. Text overlaid with contrast (text shadow, overlay gradient, or contained text block). Minimal UI elements.
**Copy focus:** Single bold headline (5-8 words), brief tagline, CTA button.
**Best for:** Brand awareness, top-of-funnel campaigns, emotional engagement.
**Example:** A sweeping landscape with a product in context, headline: "Built for teams that move fast."

### 4. Editorial Still

Magazine-quality composition with mood lighting and lifestyle context. The product is part of a larger scene.

**Visual direction:** Styled composition with intentional color palette, natural or studio lighting, environmental storytelling. Product is present but not isolated.
**Copy focus:** Minimal — let the image speak. Brand name, subtle CTA.
**Best for:** Premium brands, Instagram feed ads, brand building, audience warming.
**Example:** A laptop on a marble desk with a coffee cup and plants, soft morning light, with a subtle brand watermark.

### 5. Shoppable Tag

Product shown in context (lifestyle setting) with clickable hotspot tags pointing to specific products or features.

**Visual direction:** Lifestyle photography with clean tag overlays pointing to products within the scene. Tags include product name and price.
**Copy focus:** Product names, prices, "Tap to shop" instruction.
**Best for:** E-commerce, multi-product showcases, interactive ads, Instagram shopping.
**Example:** A living room scene with tags on the lamp ($89), throw pillow ($45), and side table ($199).

### 6. Collection

Multiple products presented in a curated grid with a cohesive visual theme and collection narrative.

**Visual direction:** Grid layout (2x2, 3x1, or collage), consistent background and lighting across all products, collection title prominent.
**Copy focus:** Collection name, theme, number of items, entry price point.
**Best for:** New collection launches, gift guides, seasonal assortments, "complete the look."
**Example:** "The Modern Workspace Collection" with 4 products in a 2x2 grid, each on a matching pale background.

### 7. UGC Hook

User-generated content style — authentic, raw, social proof-driven. Looks like organic content, not a polished ad.

**Visual direction:** Phone-quality camera, natural lighting, selfie or handheld perspective, real person using/reviewing the product. Minimal post-production.
**Copy focus:** Testimonial or first-person narrative, "real customer" attribution, authentic language.
**Best for:** Social media ads (especially TikTok, Instagram Reels), trust building, overcoming ad fatigue.
**Example:** A real user doing a selfie-style video: "I tried [product] for 30 days — here's what happened..."

### 8. Product Demo

Product in active use, showing functionality. Can be step-by-step or capturing a key moment.

**Visual direction:** Clear view of the product in action, hands or screen interaction visible, annotated callouts optional. Can be video or sequential stills.
**Copy focus:** Feature highlight, "how it works," step indicators, benefit outcome.
**Best for:** Mid-funnel education, feature-specific campaigns, onboarding ads, retargeting.
**Example:** A screen recording of the dashboard with callouts: "Step 1: Import contacts → Step 2: Build segment → Step 3: Launch campaign."

### 9. Before/After

Transformation comparison showing the state before and after using the product or service. Split screen or slider.

**Visual direction:** Side-by-side or vertical split, clear "before" and "after" labels, dramatic transformation visible. Consistent framing between the two states.
**Copy focus:** "Before: [problem state]. After: [solution state]." Quantified improvement if possible.
**Best for:** Results-oriented products, fitness, beauty, SaaS dashboards (messy spreadsheet → clean dashboard), process optimization.
**Example:** Left: chaotic spreadsheet of customer data. Right: clean Intempt dashboard with lifecycle segments. "Before: data chaos. After: customer clarity."

### 10. Value Prop

Benefit-led layout with icon + headline + proof point for each value proposition. Information-dense but organized.

**Visual direction:** Clean grid or vertical stack of benefit blocks. Each block has an icon/illustration, short headline, and one-line proof. Brand colors as accent.
**Copy focus:** 3-4 benefit headlines with supporting stats or proof points.
**Best for:** Mid-funnel consideration, feature comparison pages, LinkedIn ads, email content blocks.
**Example:** Three columns: "2x Pipeline Velocity" (icon + stat), "40% Less Manual Work" (icon + stat), "99.9% Uptime" (icon + stat).

### 11. Utility Display

Technical specifications, comparison charts, and decision-support information. Data-rich, functional.

**Visual direction:** Clean data visualization, comparison tables, spec sheets, infographics. Professional and informative.
**Copy focus:** Specifications, feature lists, comparison data, performance metrics.
**Best for:** Technical audiences, bottom-of-funnel decision support, product comparison, spec sheets.
**Example:** A feature comparison table showing your product vs. 2 competitors across 10 features with checkmarks and X marks.

### 12. Social Proof

Testimonial, review, or rating centered with the customer's face and quote prominently displayed.

**Visual direction:** Large customer photo, prominent quote in large type, star rating, company logo, role/title attribution. Clean background.
**Copy focus:** Direct customer quote (1-2 sentences), attribution (name, title, company), quantified result if available.
**Best for:** Bottom-of-funnel trust building, retargeting, landing pages, email social proof sections.
**Example:** Large headshot of Sarah Chen, VP Marketing at Acme: "Intempt doubled our conversion rate in 60 days. The ROI was clear in week one." — 5 stars, Acme logo.

### 13. Founder Story

Personal narrative from the founder or team. Behind-the-scenes, mission-driven, authentic.

**Visual direction:** Candid founder photo, informal setting (workshop, early office, product development), personal and approachable.
**Copy focus:** Origin story, mission statement, why the company exists, personal motivation.
**Best for:** Top-of-funnel brand building, early-stage company marketing, values-driven audiences, LinkedIn.
**Example:** Founder in their garage/early office: "We started Intempt because we believed every team deserves enterprise-grade engagement tools — not just the Fortune 500."

### 14. Comparison

Side-by-side comparison against a competitor or the status quo. Decision-support focused.

**Visual direction:** Two-column layout, clear "us vs. them" or "old way vs. new way" framing, checkmarks vs. X marks, your product highlighted.
**Copy focus:** Feature-by-feature comparison, pricing comparison, "why switch" messaging.
**Best for:** Bottom-of-funnel competitive displacement, comparison shopping searches, retargeting competitor visitors.
**Example:** Two columns: "Manual Process" vs. "With Intempt" — showing time savings, error reduction, and cost comparison.

## Funnel Stage Mapping

### Top of Funnel (TOF) — Awareness

| Angle | Why It Works at TOF |
|-------|-------------------|
| Full-Bleed Hero | Maximum visual impact, stops the scroll, builds brand recognition |
| UGC Hook | Feels organic, builds trust, blends into social feed |
| Founder Story | Humanizes the brand, creates emotional connection, shareable |

### Middle of Funnel (MOF) — Consideration

| Angle | Why It Works at MOF |
|-------|-------------------|
| Product Demo | Educates on capabilities, builds understanding of value |
| Before/After | Shows tangible transformation, bridges problem to solution |
| Comparison | Helps evaluate against alternatives, supports decision-making |
| Value Prop | Summarizes key benefits, reinforces value proposition |

### Bottom of Funnel (BOF) — Decision

| Angle | Why It Works at BOF |
|-------|-------------------|
| Product Card | Direct, no-nonsense product presentation for ready buyers |
| Price Drop | Creates urgency, removes price objection, drives immediate action |
| Shoppable Tag | Reduces friction from interest to purchase |
| Social Proof | Provides final validation from peers, overcomes last-mile objections |

## Creative Brief Template

Every creative asset starts with a brief that specifies these fields:

### Required Fields

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Angle** | One of the 14 creative angles | "Before/After" |
| **Channel** | Target platform | "LinkedIn Single Image" |
| **Funnel Stage** | TOF, MOF, or BOF | "MOF — Consideration" |
| **Messaging Hierarchy** | | |
| — Headline | Primary message (5-10 words) | "Stop guessing. Start knowing." |
| — Subline | Supporting message (10-20 words) | "See how Intempt turns raw data into actionable customer segments." |
| — Proof | Evidence or social proof | "Trusted by 2,000+ growth teams" |
| — CTA | Call to action (2-5 words) | "See It in Action" |
| **Visual Direction** | Describe the desired visual style | "Split screen: left side = messy spreadsheet (desaturated), right side = clean Intempt dashboard (vibrant). Professional, clean." |
| **Placement Specs** | Dimensions, file format, size limits | "1200x627px, JPG, <150KB, LinkedIn Single Image" |

### Optional Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Target Audience** | Specific persona or segment this creative targets |
| **Tone** | Emotional register (e.g., confident, empathetic, urgent) |
| **Color Palette** | Specific colors or palette reference |
| **Photography Style** | Film type, lighting, composition notes |
| **Typography** | Font preferences, size hierarchy |
| **Brand Elements** | Logo placement, brand marks, required legal text |
| **Variations Needed** | Number of variants for testing |
| **Due Date** | Production deadline |
