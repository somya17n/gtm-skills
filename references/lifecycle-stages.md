# Customer Lifecycle Stages

Intempt uses a 6-stage customer lifecycle model built on RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) scoring combined with behavioral signals. Every person record carries a lifecycle stage, behavioral scores, and intent indicators that power segmentation, journey triggers, and personalization.

---

## 6 Lifecycle Stages

| Stage | Description | Typical RFM Profile | Engagement Posture |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **At Risk** | Previously active customers showing declining engagement. Have not interacted in an extended period and may churn without intervention. | Low recency, any frequency/monetary | Urgent re-engagement: win-back campaigns, personal outreach, incentives |
| **Needs Attention** | Customers whose engagement is fading but have not fully disengaged. Still reachable but trending downward. | Medium-low recency, medium frequency | Proactive nudge: check-in emails, feature highlights, satisfaction surveys |
| **New Customers** | Recently acquired customers in their first engagement window. High recency but limited history. | High recency, low frequency, low monetary | Onboarding: welcome sequences, activation guidance, quick wins |
| **Promising** | Customers showing positive early signals — increasing frequency or monetary value. Likely to become regulars. | High recency, medium frequency, growing monetary | Nurture: education content, product adoption, community invitations |
| **Regulars** | Consistent, reliable customers with steady engagement patterns. Backbone of revenue. | Medium-high recency, high frequency, medium-high monetary | Retain: loyalty rewards, exclusive access, feedback loops |
| **Champions** | Highest-value customers with exceptional engagement across all dimensions. Most likely to refer and expand. | High recency, high frequency, high monetary | Celebrate: VIP treatment, referral programs, co-creation, advocacy |

## RFM Scoring Methodology

Each customer is scored on three dimensions using a 1-5 scale. The composite RFM score determines lifecycle stage placement.

### Recency Score

How recently the customer last engaged (purchase, login, meaningful interaction).

| Score | Label | Definition |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 5 | Very Recent | Last activity within 1-7 days |
| 4 | Recent | Last activity within 8-14 days |
| 3 | Moderate | Last activity within 15-30 days |
| 2 | Lapsing | Last activity within 31-60 days |
| 1 | Dormant | Last activity more than 60 days ago |

### Frequency Score

How often the customer engages within the measurement window (typically 90 days).

| Score | Label | Definition |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 5 | Very Frequent | 20+ interactions in window |
| 4 | Frequent | 12-19 interactions in window |
| 3 | Moderate | 6-11 interactions in window |
| 2 | Infrequent | 2-5 interactions in window |
| 1 | Rare | 0-1 interactions in window |

### Monetary Score

Revenue or value generated by the customer within the measurement window.

| Score | Label | Definition |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 5 | Top Tier | Top 10% by revenue |
| 4 | High | Top 10-25% by revenue |
| 3 | Medium | Top 25-50% by revenue |
| 2 | Low | Top 50-75% by revenue |
| 1 | Minimal | Bottom 25% by revenue |

### RFM-to-Stage Mapping

| RFM Pattern | Assigned Stage |
|-------------|---------------|
| R=5, F=5, M=5 | Champions |
| R=5, F=5, M=4 | Champions |
| R=5, F=4, M=5 | Champions |
| R=4-5, F=4-5, M=3-5 | Regulars |
| R=4-5, F=3-4, M=3-4 | Regulars |
| R=4-5, F=2-3, M=2-3 | Promising |
| R=5, F=1, M=1 | New Customers |
| R=4-5, F=1-2, M=1-2 | New Customers |
| R=2-3, F=2-4, M=2-4 | Needs Attention |
| R=2-3, F=3-5, M=3-5 | Needs Attention |
| R=1-2, F=1-5, M=1-5 | At Risk |
| R=1, F=4-5, M=4-5 | At Risk |

## Behavioral Score Definitions

Beyond RFM, Intempt computes two behavioral scores and an intent level for each person.

### Activity Score (0-100)

Measures the breadth and depth of a customer's engagement across tracked events.

| Range | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 80-100 | Highly Active | Engaging across multiple channels and features daily |
| 60-79 | Active | Regular engagement with core features |
| 40-59 | Moderately Active | Periodic engagement, using some features |
| 20-39 | Low Activity | Sporadic engagement, limited feature use |
| 0-19 | Inactive | Little to no measurable engagement |

**Scoring inputs:** page views (weight: 10%), feature usage (30%), event triggers (25%), session duration (15%), login frequency (20%).

### Fit Score (0-100)

Measures how well a customer matches the ideal customer profile (ICP) based on firmographic and demographic attributes.

| Range | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 80-100 | Excellent Fit | Matches ICP across all key dimensions |
| 60-79 | Good Fit | Matches most ICP criteria |
| 40-59 | Moderate Fit | Partial ICP match |
| 20-39 | Weak Fit | Few ICP attributes match |
| 0-19 | Poor Fit | Does not match ICP |

**Scoring inputs:** company size (20%), industry (20%), role/title (20%), technology stack (15%), geography (10%), budget range (15%).

### Intent Level

Derived from behavioral signals that indicate purchase or expansion readiness.

| Level | Description | Typical Signals |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **High** | Actively evaluating or ready to buy/expand | Pricing page visits, demo requests, feature comparison, proposal downloads, trial activation |
| **Medium** | Showing interest but not yet in buying mode | Content consumption, webinar attendance, multiple sessions, returning visits |
| **Low** | Minimal purchase signals | Occasional visits, passive browsing, no conversion-oriented actions |

---

## Intent Trend Indicators

Intent trends are computed by comparing intent signals across three time windows.

| Trend | Definition | Calculation |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Rising** | Intent score increasing over time | 7-day average > 14-day average > 30-day average |
| **Steady** | Intent score stable | Less than 10% variance across all three windows |
| **Declining** | Intent score decreasing over time | 7-day average < 14-day average < 30-day average |

**Window definitions:**
- **7-day window:** Rolling average of daily intent signals over the last 7 days
- **14-day window:** Rolling average of daily intent signals over the last 14 days
- **30-day window:** Rolling average of daily intent signals over the last 30 days

The trend is determined by comparing adjacent windows. A difference of greater than 10% between windows is considered significant.

## Segment Filter Operators

Segments are built using filters with the following operators:

| Operator | Symbol | Description | Example |
|----------|--------|-------------|---------|
| Equals | `=` | Exact match | `lifecycle_stage = "Champions"` |
| Not equals | `!=` | Excludes exact match | `lifecycle_stage != "At Risk"` |
| In | `in` | Matches any value in a list | `lifecycle_stage in ["Regulars", "Champions"]` |
| Not in | `not_in` | Excludes all values in a list | `lifecycle_stage not_in ["At Risk", "Needs Attention"]` |
| Greater than | `>` | Numeric comparison | `activity_score > 60` |
| Less than | `<` | Numeric comparison | `fit_score < 40` |
| Greater or equal | `>=` | Numeric comparison | `monetary_score >= 3` |
| Less or equal | `<=` | Numeric comparison | `recency_score <= 2` |
| Contains | `contains` | Substring match (strings) | `email contains "@enterprise.com"` |
| Within | `within` | Temporal proximity | `last_activity within 7d` |

### Temporal Filters

Time-based filters use the `within` operator with duration units:

| Unit | Syntax | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| Days | `Xd` | `last_purchase within 30d` |
| Weeks | `Xw` | `first_visit within 4w` |
| Months | `Xm` | `created_at within 3m` |

Temporal filters always evaluate relative to the current timestamp at query time.

## Stage Transition Rules

Stage transitions follow defined thresholds and are evaluated on a configurable schedule (default: daily).

### Transition Triggers

| From | To | Condition |
|------|----|-----------|
| New Customers | Promising | frequency_score >= 2 AND monetary_score >= 2 AND days_since_first_activity > 14 |
| New Customers | Needs Attention | recency_score <= 2 AND days_since_first_activity > 30 |
| Promising | Regulars | frequency_score >= 4 AND recency_score >= 4 AND days_since_first_activity > 30 |
| Promising | Needs Attention | recency_score <= 2 |
| Regulars | Champions | recency_score = 5 AND frequency_score >= 4 AND monetary_score >= 4 |
| Regulars | Needs Attention | recency_score <= 3 AND frequency_score declining |
| Champions | Regulars | recency_score <= 3 OR monetary_score <= 3 |
| Needs Attention | At Risk | recency_score = 1 AND no_activity_days > 45 |
| Needs Attention | Promising | recency_score >= 4 AND frequency_score >= 3 |
| At Risk | Needs Attention | recency_score >= 3 (re-engagement detected) |
| At Risk | New Customers | Not allowed (must go through Needs Attention first) |

### Transition Cooldown

To prevent stage flapping, a minimum dwell time of 7 days is enforced before a customer can transition again. Exceptions:
- Transition to "At Risk" has no cooldown (immediate on threshold breach)
- Manual stage overrides bypass cooldown

## Real-World Lifecycle Benchmarks (2024–2025)

Data from Mixpanel, Amplitude, Pendo, and Braze. These help calibrate what healthy, average, and at-risk look like in practice.

### Engagement Benchmarks — SaaS

| Metric | Low | Average | Good | Best-in-Class | Source |
|--------|-----|---------|------|--------------|--------|
| DAU/MAU (stickiness ratio) | < 10% | 16% median | 25–35% | 40%+ | Amplitude 2024 |
| DAU/MAU — B2B SaaS | — | **31%** | — | 50%+ | Mixpanel 2024 benchmark |
| WAU/MAU (weekly active) | < 30% | 40–50% | 55–65% | 75%+ | Amplitude 2024 |
| Day 7 retention | < 20% | 25–35% | 35–45% | 55%+ | Amplitude / Mixpanel 2024 |
| Day 30 retention | < 10% | 15–25% | 25–35% | 45%+ | Amplitude 2024 |
| Month 3 retention | < 5% | 10–20% | 20–30% | 40%+ | Lenny's Newsletter 2024 |

**Context:** DAU/MAU of 25% is the "good" threshold for B2B SaaS. Products below 16% (the median) have a product-market fit or engagement problem, not just a marketing problem.

### Lifecycle Stage Distribution — Typical SaaS

What a healthy vs. struggling distribution looks like across a customer base:

| Stage | Healthy Distribution | Struggling Distribution | Notes |
|-------|---------------------|------------------------|-------|
| New Customers | 10–15% | 25–35% | High % = churning customers, need replacement |
| Promising | 15–20% | 10–15% | Low % = poor activation, users not progressing |
| Regulars | 30–40% | 15–25% | Core revenue base — low % = retention problem |
| Champions | 10–20% | 5–10% | Advocacy engine — low % = upsell/expansion stalled |
| Needs Attention | 10–15% | 20–30% | High % = product-market fit signal to investigate |
| At Risk | 5–10% | 15–25% | High % = active churn risk — win-back urgently |

Source: Pendo 2024 SaaS Benchmark Report, Mixpanel 2024.

## Default Scoring Weights

When no custom configuration is provided, these weights apply:

| Component | Weight in Overall Score |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Recency | 35% |
| Frequency | 30% |
| Monetary | 35% |

| Behavioral Component | Weight |
|----------------------|--------|
| Activity Score | 50% |
| Fit Score | 30% |
| Intent Level | 20% |

These weights are configurable per project. Adjusting weights automatically recalculates all customer stages on the next evaluation cycle.
